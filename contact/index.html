Safety first

Gas Smell or Sewage Backup? Call These Numbers First

Some emergencies need a public agency before a plumber.

1

Gas smell or danger

Leave the building, do not touch switches, and call 911 from outside. Then call us once everyone is safe.

2

Sewer backup

The City asks residents to call Suisun City Police Dispatch at 707-421-7373, available 24/7, so the right crew is sent. Then call us to clear and inspect your private lateral.

3

Water service questions

To start or change water service, the City Finance Department can help. Our plumbers handle the pipes inside and on your property.

If you are unsure whether your problem is public or private, ask when you call and the dispatcher will explain.
