Why a Plumbing Failure Costs a Business More Than the Repair

When a home loses water, the family waits. When a shop or restaurant loses water, the register stops, staff go home and perishable stock is at risk. That is why commercial calls get sorted by what is keeping you closed, not by the size of the pipe.

Our dispatch line is open around the clock, so a Saturday night grease overflow gets the same attention as a Tuesday morning leak. Tell the dispatcher whether you own the building or lease it, because that decides who can approve the work.

What We Fix for Shops, Restaurants, Offices and Rentals

Most business calls fall into a short list of problems. These are the ones that come up most.

  • ✓
    Restrooms. Running or clogged toilets, leaking flush valves, dripping lavatory faucets and urinal drains that back up.
  • ✓
    Kitchen drains and grease lines. Slow prep sinks, floor drains that gurgle and grease-laden lines that need cabling or jetting.
  • ✓
    Water heaters. Tank units that leak or fail, and larger heaters that no longer keep up with a busy dish station.
  • ✓
    Supply leaks. Pinholes in copper, failed shutoff valves and leaks above ceiling tiles that stain before they drip.
  • ✓
    Main line backups. Sewage rising in the lowest drain, which usually points to the building lateral rather than a single fixture.
  • ✓
    Backflow assemblies and storm drains. Testing, repairs and clearing of roof and yard drains before the rainy season.

If your problem is on the list but needs a deeper look, we can run a camera through the line first. The sewer camera inspection page explains what the footage shows.

Burst Pipe or Backup in Your Business? Do This While You Wait

We are open 24/7. These steps protect your stock, your floors and your people until the plumber walks in.

1

Find the main shutoff

Close the building main or the valve feeding the affected area. Ask any manager now where it is, not during the flood.

2

Stop using drains

No flushing, no dish machine, no mop sink. Every gallon you send down a blocked line ends up on the floor.

3

Cut power near water

If water is near outlets, kitchen equipment or a panel, leave the area and shut breakers only if you can reach them dry.

4

Move stock and records

Lift boxes, paper files and dry goods off the floor. Photograph the damage for your insurer.

5

Tell us who approves work

Give the dispatcher the owner or property manager contact if you are a tenant. It saves a second phone call.

For a gas smell, leave the building and call 911 first. Gas is never a plumbing-call-first situation.

Restaurants and Grease: Keeping the Kitchen Line Moving

Kitchen drains fail in a predictable way. Fats cool inside the pipe, harden into a collar and shrink the opening a little more each week until a busy Friday finishes the job.

Cable now or jet later

A cable can punch a hole through the clog and restore flow for the shift. If the line keeps closing after cabling, the walls still hold a grease coat, and hydro jetting scours it off with high-pressure water.

Traps need a schedule

A grease trap or interceptor only works while it is emptied on a regular cycle. If yours smells or the sink drains slowly after each cleaning, say so on the call. It may mean the trap is undersized or the outlet line is partly blocked. Grease interceptors and backflow protection on commercial kitchens are governed by the California Plumbing Code, which is built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), so sizing and placement should follow that code and not habit.

Local note

Floodwater, Sewer Laterals and the Buildings on Suisun City's Low Ground

Suisun City sits about 6.6 feet above sea level beside the marsh, and the community has flooded during storms and high tides. For a business, that means a sewer backup after heavy rain is a real possibility, so know where your cleanout is and keep stock off the floor in the lowest room.

The City owns the smaller sewer mains, but the lateral from your building to the main belongs to the property owner. If sewage is rising, the City asks you to call Police Dispatch at 707-421-7373 so the right crew is sent, then call us for the private side of the line.

Source: City of Suisun City Sewer & Wastewater page and the Fairfield-Suisun Sewer District FAQ.

What Moves the Price of a Commercial Plumbing Job

We do not quote numbers on a web page because every building is different. These are the factors that change the figure you hear on the phone.

Access. A cleanout in a back hallway is quick. A line under a finished floor or behind a fixed wall adds labor.
Hour of the call. Night and weekend visits cost more than a scheduled weekday visit, so planned work usually costs less.
Parts and equipment. Commercial valves, flush valves and heaters differ in size and availability.
Cause. A hair-and-paper clog is simpler than roots in a lateral or a collapsed section.

You get a clear price before work starts, and you can say no if it does not fit. Call the 24/7 line for your real number.
