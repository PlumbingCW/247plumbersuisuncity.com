Humming, Silent, Leaking or Smelly: Read the Symptom

A disposal usually tells you what is wrong before you take anything apart.

Hums but nothing turns

The grinding plate is jammed, often by a bone, fruit pit or bottle cap. The motor is working, and it overheats if you keep running it.

Dead silent

The unit tripped its reset button, lost power at the outlet or the motor has burned out. Only the last one means replacement.

Water under the sink

Leaks come from the sink flange, the dishwasher hose, the drain outlet or the housing itself. Where it drips from decides the repair.

Bad smell or slow drain

Food residue coats the chamber and the pipe behind it. The cause may be the disposal or the line downstream.

Disposal Leaking Under the Sink? Do This Before We Arrive

We are open 24/7. A few steps keep the cabinet dry and make the visit shorter.

1

Switch it off

Turn off the wall switch and unplug the unit if the cord is under the sink and the floor is dry.

2

Stop the water

Close the sink's hot and cold stops, or the main if they stick. Don't run the dishwasher.

3

Empty the cabinet

Move cleaners and bags out and set a bucket under the drip. Wet particleboard swells fast.

4

Never reach inside

Hands stay out of the chamber, even when it looks off. Use tongs for anything visible.

5

Note where it drips

Tell the dispatcher whether the drip comes from the top, the side or the hose. It speeds up the plan.

If the water coming up is gray and smells like sewage, stop all use of the kitchen and describe that on the call. A kitchen line backed up to the sink is a drain job, not a disposal job.

What Is Safe to Try and What Is Not

A jam is the one disposal problem most people can handle, if they do it carefully.

Safe to try

  • Press the red reset button on the bottom
  • Turn the jammed blades by hand with the hex key socket
  • Remove visible objects with tongs
  • Run cold water with the unit on after clearing

Leave to the plumber

  • Leaks at the housing or flange
  • A burned-out motor
  • Chemical drain cleaners in the chamber
  • Any work inside while the unit has power

Chemical cleaners damage the seals and splash back when someone opens the drain later. They also do not work well on grease.

Why the Disposal Gets Blamed for a Clogged Sink

A kitchen drain collects grease, coffee grounds and starch. The disposal chops the food small, but the pieces still have to travel down a narrow pipe, and fats harden along the walls.

If the basin drains slowly with the unit off, the clog is past the disposal. Our drain cleaning service cables the line, and hydro jetting is the next step if it keeps coming back.

How the Dishwasher Connects to the Disposal

Most dishwashers drain through a hose that clips to a port on the side of the disposal. If the disposal is new, a knockout plug inside that port has to be removed or the dishwasher won't drain.

A loose hose clamp or a clogged port causes the leak many homeowners think is the disposal. A new unit or a remodeled sink also calls for a proper hookup, which falls under plumbing installation. The California Plumbing Code, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), requires the dishwasher drain to be set up with a high loop or air gap so dirty sink water cannot flow back into the dishwasher.

When a New Disposal Is the Better Choice

If the housing leaks or the motor has burned out, replacing the whole unit is the repair. Housings can't be patched because the leak is usually a rusted seam.

Replacement also makes sense when the old unit jams on every other use. A new one with a more powerful motor often costs less than repeated service calls. The plumber will compare both options on the phone.

What Affects the Price of Disposal Work

A reset and a new unit are very different calls. These factors move the figure.

Repair or replace. Clearing a jam takes minutes. A new unit needs the old one removed and the plumbing reconnected.
Motor size. Larger motors grind tougher waste and cost more up front.
Cabinet condition. Water damage under the sink may need to be dealt with before a new unit is set.
Hour of the call. After-hours visits cost more than scheduled daytime visits.
