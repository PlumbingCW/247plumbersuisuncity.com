Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Suisun City 24/7 Plumbing handles when you call us, email us or visit this website. Effective October 15, 2026.

Who We Are

Suisun City 24/7 Plumbing operates this website and a 24/7 phone dispatch line from 333 Sunset Ave, Suisun City, CA 94585.

This site has no online forms, accounts or checkout. Requests for service are taken by phone.

Information We Receive

When you call, we may ask for your name, address, a callback number and a description of the plumbing problem. We use this to send the right plumber and to schedule the visit.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write.

  • Details you give during a phone call
  • Details you include in an email
  • Standard web server logs, such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and time of visit

How We Use Information

We use your details to answer your request, dispatch a plumber, explain the price and follow up on the job. Web server logs help us keep the site running and spot technical problems.

We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the plumber or crew assigned to your job, with people who help us run the service, or where the law requires.

Third-Party Services

Pages may embed a map from a third-party provider, and the provider may receive technical data from your browser when the map loads. Links to outside sites, such as government pages, are covered by those sites' own policies.

Keeping and Protecting Information

We keep job details for as long as needed to complete the work and handle follow-up questions. We take reasonable steps to protect what we hold, but no system is completely secure.

Your Choices and How to Reach Us

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it. Email contact@247plumbersuisuncity.com or use the details on our contact page.

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.

Plumbing Trouble Right Now?

The dispatch line is open every hour of every day. Prefer to talk it through? Call (916) 312-0578 and a dispatcher answers. You can also reach us on the main line at (916) 312-0578 at any hour.
