How to Check Any Plumber's License

Do this with us and with anyone else you are thinking of hiring. It takes about two minutes.

  • ✓
    Use the state tool. Open the License Check tool at cslb.ca.gov, or call the California Contractors State License Board at (800) 321-2752.
  • ✓
    Search by name or number. Confirm the license is active and that the classification covers plumbing work.
  • ✓
    Do not trust a printed number alone. The board warns that a license number on an ad or a contract does not prove it is valid.
  • ✓
    Ask for it on paper. A legitimate plumber will give you the license details and explain the price before starting.

How to Read a Plumbing Quote

A good quote names the work, the main parts and the labor in plain language. If a price is a single number with no detail, ask what it covers. You should also ask whether anything could change it, such as a hidden leak found once a wall is open.

Compare quotes on scope, not just the bottom line. A lower number that leaves out a shutoff valve, a haul-away or bracing may cost more by the end of the job. We explain each item so you can compare fairly with any other plumber.

Water Heater Bracing: What California Requires

California Health and Safety Code section 19211 requires new and replacement water heaters, and existing residential ones, to be braced, anchored or strapped against earthquake motion. It applies to standard units of 120 gallons or less, and sellers certify compliance to buyers.

For you, that means a loose heater is a safety and a sale issue. Our water heater installation and replacement work includes bracing.
