Slow Drain or Full Backup? Read the Symptoms First

How the water behaves tells you a lot about where the blockage sits and how fast you need help.

One sink drains slowly

Hair, soap film and grease build up in the trap and the short pipe behind it. This is the easiest clog to clear and usually a cable job.

Water rises in the tub when the washer runs

The two fixtures share a branch line. A clog downstream of both is holding back water from everything upstream.

Toilet gurgles when a sink drains

Air is being pushed through the wrong path because the line is partly blocked. Treat it as an early warning, not a quirk.

Wastewater shows up in the lowest drain

A shower or floor drain that fills with dirty water points toward the main line. Stop using water and call right away.

Backed Up Right Now? Do This Before We Arrive

These steps cost nothing and keep a bad clog from turning into a flooded room.

1

Stop all water use

No flushing, no dishwasher, no laundry. Every gallon you add has nowhere to go but the floor.

2

Skip the chemical drain opener

Caustic liquids sit in a standing clog and burn skin when the plumber opens the trap. Tell the dispatcher if you already poured some in.

3

Pull up rugs and move belongings

Clear the area around the overflowing fixture so water damage stays on hard flooring.

4

Note which fixtures are affected

Knowing whether it is one drain or several helps the plumber bring the right equipment.

How a Stubborn Clog Gets Cleared

Most drain jobs follow the same path, from the fixture outward.

1

Find the blockage

The plumber tests nearby fixtures and listens to how the line drains to decide where the cable should enter.

2

Feed the cable

A motorized cable with a cutting head goes in through a cleanout or the fixture trap. It bores through the clog and scrapes the pipe wall.

3

Test the flow

Running water through the line confirms it moves freely and shows whether the clog was a one-time event.

4

Explain what was found

If the cable pulls back roots or heavy scale, you hear about better options before any added work begins.

Plunger and Hand Auger Versus a Professional Cable Machine

Home tools clear light clogs. They stop helping when the blockage sits farther down the line.

Home tools work when

  • The clog is within a few feet of the drain opening
  • Water drains slowly but steadily
  • Only one fixture is affected
  • The blockage broke up on the first or second try

Call a plumber when

  • The clog returns within days
  • Two or more fixtures back up together
  • A cable meets resistance it cannot pass
  • You hear gurgling from drains you did not use

A hand auger pushed too hard can scratch old pipe or jam a clog tighter. The California Plumbing Code, built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), expects each fixture to have a trap and a vent, so a gurgling drain can point to a blocked vent as well as a clogged pipe. A repeat clog is a sign to stop guessing and have the line looked at.

Hard Water and Older Plumbing Make Clogs Stick

Suisun City tap water measured 170 ppm hardness in the City's 2025 water quality report, which falls in the hard band on the common USGS scale. Minerals from hard water leave a rough film inside drain pipe, and hair and grease catch on that film. If your drains slow down again a few weeks after a cleaning, scale may be part of the story.

Much of the housing in town dates from the late 1970s through the 1990s, so many drain lines have been carrying scale and soap for decades. That does not mean your pipes are failing. It does mean a camera check is a sensible step when the same line keeps clogging.

Habits That Keep Drains Moving

A few small changes cut down on repeat visits.

  • ✓
    Wipe pans before washing. Pour cooled grease into a can and throw it out with the trash.
  • ✓
    Fit a hair catcher. A simple mesh screen in the tub and shower stops most hair clogs.
  • ✓
    Flush only toilet paper. Wipes labeled flushable still tangle in pipes and snag on roots.
  • ✓
    Run hot water after dishes. It carries loose residue past the trap before it cools and sticks.

For recurring buildup that a cable cannot fully remove, hydro jetting washes the pipe wall clean. If every drain in the house is slow, ask about sewer line cleaning.
