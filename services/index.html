Which Service Do You Actually Need?

Symptoms overlap, so here is a quick way to sort them. If none of these fit, call and describe what you notice.

Water that is cold or lukewarm

Start with water heater repair. If the tank is leaking at the base, skip ahead to water heater replacement.

One drain slow, the rest fine

A local clog. Drain cleaning usually clears it, and hydro jetting handles lines that keep coming back.

Every drain gurgles or backs up

That points to the main line. See sewer line cleaning, and consider a sewer camera inspection to find the cause.

High water bill, no visible leak

Something is leaking out of sight. Leak detection locates it, and the repair might be slab leak repair or water line repair.

Emergency Call or Scheduled Visit?

Some problems can wait for a planned appointment. Others cannot. Here is how to tell the difference.

Plan a scheduled visit

  • A faucet that drips slowly into a sink
  • A toilet that runs now and then
  • A remodel with new fixtures
  • A water softener or tankless upgrade
  • A sewer camera check before buying a home

Call the 24/7 line now

  • Water spraying or pooling that will not stop
  • Sewage coming up in a tub, shower or floor drain
  • A split water heater tank
  • Any smell of gas (call 911 first)
  • No water at all to the house

Not sure which column you are in? Call anyway. Same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows, and a plumber can tell you over the phone whether it can wait.

What to Do First, Whatever the Problem

Find your main water shutoff before you need it. Every job we do follows the California Plumbing Code, which is built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), so for you it means the work meets a statewide standard and the Building Division is the place to ask about permits.
