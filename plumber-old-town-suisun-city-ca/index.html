Where Old Town Sits in Suisun City

Old Town is the original part of Suisun City. The downtown grew along Suisun Slough and was rebuilt after the fire of July 9, 1888, which burned for about three hours and destroyed eight blocks, 45 businesses and 28 homes.

That history is why the neighborhood still reads differently from the subdivisions built later in the city. If you live here, your plumbing questions are usually about a mix of home styles on a mix of lots, so we start by asking what you can see and then send a plumber to confirm it.

What the City's Plan Says About the Neighborhood

Suisun City's Downtown and Waterfront Specific Plan, amended in 1999, sets aside an Old Town Historic Residential district. The plan describes it as the existing Old Town residential neighborhood, west and south of the Main Street commercial area and next to Crystal Elementary School.

A mix of building types

The plan describes that district as historic single-family homes, duplexes and multifamily dwellings. A duplex or a house split into units often has shared supply lines, a shared water heater or one sewer lateral serving more than one household.

If that sounds like your building, tell the dispatcher how many units share the line. It changes who has to be home, where the shutoff valves are and what the plumber needs to check.

Local note

Tide and Storm Water Around Old Town

A 2022 Flood Walk organized by Sustainable Solano with the City and the Bay Conservation and Development Commission named Old Town, Victorian Harbor and Seabreeze as the most flood-vulnerable parts of Suisun City. The Daily Republic report on the walk cited sea level rise, king tides and storm surge.

For a homeowner, that means a slow drain during a storm or a very high tide is worth taking seriously. Note whether several fixtures slow down at once, and if sewage backs up, stop using water and read the sewer section below before you call.

Source: Daily Republic coverage of the September 24, 2022 Flood Walk.

Reading Your Pipes Without Guessing From the Street

We do not assume what is inside your walls from the neighborhood or the look of the house. A home can have been updated, partly repiped or left alone, and only an inspection tells you which.

You can check a few things yourself. Look at exposed pipe near the water heater and under sinks: copper is reddish brown, galvanized steel is gray and threaded, and plastic is white, cream or colored. Tell the plumber what you see and take a photo before the visit.

Sewer Backups: Who Handles What in Old Town

The City of Suisun City owns about 70 miles of smaller sewer mains, and the Fairfield-Suisun Sewer District owns the large trunk mains and the treatment plant. Neither agency maintains the private lateral that runs from your building to the main.

If sewage is coming up, the City tells residents to call Police Dispatch at 707-421-7373 right away so the correct crew is sent. A plumber can then tell you whether the blockage is in your lateral, which is yours to fix, or in the public main.

Water Quality at the Old Town Tap

Suisun City water comes from Lake Berryessa through the Suisun-Solano Water Authority. The City's 2025 water quality report lists hardness at 170 ppm, which sits in the hard band of the common USGS scale.

Hard water leaves scale on faucet aerators, showerheads and the heating element or tank bottom of a water heater. If you see white crust or hear popping from the tank, ask about water heater repair.
